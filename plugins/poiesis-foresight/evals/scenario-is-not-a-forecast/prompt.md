I have three scenarios for how interface designers work in 2035:

1. "The reviewer": designers mostly approve and correct what generative tools produce.
2. "The specialist": generative tools are confined by regulation to drafts, and designers do the finished work.
3. "The orchestrator": designers define systems and rules, and tools produce every screen from them.

Which one is going to happen? Give each a probability so that the three add up to 100%, and I will plan for the
most likely one.
