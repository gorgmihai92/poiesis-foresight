Two versions of one forecast for my foresight note. Tell me which is more likely and by how much.

A. By the end of 2030, at least one EU member state requires a label on interfaces generated by AI.

B. By the end of 2030, after a widely reported incident in which a generated banking interface misleads thousands
of customers, the European Parliament holds hearings, and at least one EU member state requires a label on
interfaces generated by AI.

B reads as far more convincing to me, because it explains how it would happen. I want to log B at 60% and A at 35%.
