# Drivers: the forces that matter

Which forces are shaping the subject, which of them matter most, and which are uncertain enough to build
scenarios on.

## Read first
Notes in `research/notes` of the plugin's repository.
- `go-science-2024-futures-toolkit`: its Driver Mapping section, read in full (p. 49–54), and the glossary. It is
  practitioner guidance: it gives steps and warnings, and reports no test of the method. The steps below adapt its
  three steps (Open Government Licence v3.0).

## Steps (toolkit, p. 51–53)
1. **Gather.** Start from what the project holds: the signals kept in `playbooks/scan.md`, the series read in
   `playbooks/trends.md`, insights and research notes. Add drivers the user names. A driver with no source in the
   project is marked "from general knowledge, unverified".
2. **Cover the kinds.** Sort the drivers by kind so that none is missing: political, economic, social,
   technological, legal and regulatory, environmental (p. 51). Say which kinds are empty. An empty kind is a gap
   in the input, not a finding.
3. **Word each one as a phrase that names the direction of change.** "Sustained economic growth in China" is
   clearer than "The economy" (p. 52).
4. **Rate each twice,** with one line of reason: how important it is to the scoping question, and how certain its
   outcome is. Both are judgments: label them as interpretation and give the source each rests on.
5. **Sort by the two ratings** (p. 53):
   - important and certain: act on it now; it holds in every scenario;
   - important and uncertain: a **critical uncertainty**; look into it further, or build scenarios on it;
   - less important and uncertain: track it, since it may grow in importance: a candidate signpost;
   - less important and certain: park it.
6. **Pick three to five** from the two "important" groups as the drivers that matter most (p. 53), and list the
   critical uncertainties for `playbooks/scenarios.md`.

## Rules
- The risk the toolkit names is too narrow a range of drivers (p. 50). Its remedy is a diverse group, outside
  experts or a researched list. One person working with a model has none of the three: say which kinds of source
  the drivers came from, and what was not consulted.
- "Certain" means the direction is settled, not that the driver is good or large.
- A driver is not a forecast. A driver whose outcome can be checked gets one in the log.

## Then
The ratings that carry a conclusion go through `playbooks/challenge.md`.

## Report
The driver table of `templates/foresight.md`: driver, kind, importance, certainty, what to do with it, **From**
(the ids and notes it rests on). Then the three to five that matter most, and the critical uncertainties.

## Save (see SKILL.md, "Saving")
In `foresight/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<topic>.md`, under "Drivers".
