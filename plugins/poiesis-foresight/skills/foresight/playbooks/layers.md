# Layers: uncommon conclusions

A way to read the same issue at four depths, to find conclusions that a headline does not show.

## Read first
Notes in `research/notes` of the plugin's repository.
- `inayatullah-1998-causal-layered-analysis`: **title only** in this repository. poiesis-insight's note on it is
  marked "abstract or secondary summaries only". The four layers below are worded as poiesis-insight's playbook
  words them. Nothing here rests on what the paper says: this playbook is a way to look, and what it finds is a
  lead until the challenge has passed it.

## The four readings
For the main issue, write four readings: the **litany** (the headline trend), the **social causes**, the
**worldview** behind them, the **myth or metaphor**. Conclusions that only appear at the deeper layers are the
uncommon ones; they still go through `playbooks/challenge.md`.

## Rules
- Each reading is labelled as interpretation. Each names the evidence items it starts from, with their
  references.
- Uncommon is a reason to check harder, not a reason to report.
- A reading that can be checked becomes a forecast (`playbooks/forecasts.md`) or a signpost
  (`playbooks/signposts.md`). One that cannot be checked is reported as a way of seeing, and says so.

## Save (see SKILL.md, "Saving")
In `foresight/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<topic>.md`, under "Layers".
