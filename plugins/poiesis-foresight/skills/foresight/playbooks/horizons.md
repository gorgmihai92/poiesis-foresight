# Horizons

Where each pattern sits: the way things are done now, what is emerging at the edges, and what lies between.

## Read first
Notes in `research/notes` of the plugin's repository. Each says how much of its source was read.
- `sharpe-2016-three-horizons`: read in part (p. 1, 4–5, 8, 10). A practice described by the people who
  facilitate it; not a test of whether it works.
- `go-science-2024-futures-toolkit`: its Three Horizons section, read in full (p. 42–48). The order of the steps

## The three horizons (Sharpe et al. 2016, p. 4–5)
Place each pattern: **H1** the current way, losing its fit; **H3** an emerging pattern on the fringes; **H2** the
transitional innovations, either feeding H3 (H2+) or being absorbed back into H1 (H2−). All three exist at once.

## Steps (toolkit, p. 44–47)
1. **H1 first.** What is taken for granted today in this area: how things work, who does what, what is assumed.
   For each, where does it start to lose its fit, and what shows it?
2. **H3 next.** What is emerging today that could become ordinary: the signals kept in `playbooks/scan.md` and the
   series read in `playbooks/trends.md`. For each, when might it stop being marginal? That date is a guess: write
   it as a forecast or leave it out.
3. **H2 last.** Pair one H1 feature that is being challenged with the H3 change that challenges it. List what is
   being tried in between. Mark each as H2+ (it opens the way to H3) or H2− (it props up H1), with the reason.
4. **Give each kept signal its Horizon:** H1 declining, H2+, H2− or H3 emerging, in the signal's entry.

## Rules
- A placement is a reading. Calling something H2+ or H2− "is not absolute but something that can be explored"
  (Sharpe et al. 2016, p. 8). Label it as interpretation and say what would move it.
- Every pattern is present at all times, to a greater or lesser extent (p. 5). Do not write that H1 "is over".
- The framework sorts what was found. It does not measure, and it does not replace scenarios for the
  uncertainties of the third horizon (p. 10).
- It was written for a group in dialogue. One person working with a model gets the sorting, not the dialogue.
  Say so if the result will be used to speak for others.

## Then
Placements that carry a conclusion go through `playbooks/challenge.md`.
