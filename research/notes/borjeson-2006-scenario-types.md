# Scenario types and techniques: towards a user's guide (Börjeson, Höjer, Dreborg, Ekvall & Finnveden, 2006)

- **Source:** Futures 38(7): 723–739 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.futures.2005.12.002
- **File:** none (the repository copy OpenAlex lists answered with a bot check; the publisher's site refuses)
- **Licence:** closed at the publisher.
- **Evidence:** analytical · a typology (abstract).
- **Read:** abstract only, as OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar returned it on 2026-11-22 through poiesis-research's
  `search.py`.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- The typology is based on what the user of scenarios needs to know: "what will happen, what can happen, and/or
  how a predefined target can be achieved".
- The paper discusses techniques for generating scenarios, integrating them and checking their consistency.

## What it means for the plugin
- `playbooks/scenarios.md` asks first which of the three questions the user has, because the kind of scenario
  follows from it. Only the three questions are taken, and they are quoted from the abstract. The names the paper
  gives the types, and its techniques, were not read and are not used.
