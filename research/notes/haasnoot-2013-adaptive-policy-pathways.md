## What it says (from the abstract)
- A planner should create a strategic vision of the future, commit to short-term actions, and establish a
  framework to guide future actions.
- The method joins two approaches: one with types of actions and "signposts to monitor to see if adaptation is
  needed", and one that explores sequences of possible actions over time.
- The illustration's results "suggest that it is worthwhile to further test and use the approach".
