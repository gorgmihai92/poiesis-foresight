## What it says (from the abstract)
- AI tools "can now generate numerous scenarios on seemingly any topic at essentially zero cost to the user".
- The authors hold that the usefulness of those scenarios depends on the user's ability to draw the right raw
  material from the tool, and that such tools can provide base material but are unlikely to replace the people
  who write scenarios.
- They ask whether what such a tool produces counts as scenarios by the usual definitions.

## What it means for the plugin
- A scenario a model writes is treated as a draft: `playbooks/scenarios.md` requires each one to name the drivers
  it is built on and where they came from, and the owner to choose and edit. That is a caution taken from a
  practitioners' reflection read as an abstract; it is not evidence of how good model-written scenarios are.
