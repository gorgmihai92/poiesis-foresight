## What it says (from the abstract)
- Foresight and horizon scanning are often treated as the same thing and can be quite different activities.
- "Scanning is no debate and no strategy building."
- Foresight is more process-oriented and always includes a scanning phase; scanning sits at the beginning of a
  forward-looking activity and can be an automated, stand-alone way of identifying new things.
