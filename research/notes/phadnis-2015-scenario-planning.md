# Effect of scenario planning on field experts' judgment of long-range investment decisions (Phadnis, Caplice, Sheffi & Singh, 2015)

- **Source:** Strategic Management Journal 36(9): 1401–1411 · https://doi.org/10.1002/smj.2293 (the early-view
  PDF of 2014; pages = PDF pages, 11 in all)
- **File:** none in this repository. The text read is poiesis-insight's local copy,
  `research/papers/phadnis-2015-scenario-planning-experts.txt`, which that repository keeps out of git.
- **Licence:** © John Wiley & Sons: closed.
- **Evidence:** empirical · three field studies with experts judging real long-range transport investments,
  before and after a scenario workshop, with no control group (p. 1, 4).
- **Read:** part of the text, on 2026-11-22: the abstract and introduction (p. 1) and the passages on earlier
  evidence, participants and design (p. 2–4). Not read: the hypotheses, results and discussion (p. 5–11).

## What it says
- Using multiple scenarios did not raise or lower experts' confidence overall. Their judgment of an investment
  moved with how that investment fared in the scenario they used (p. 1).
- After using multiple scenarios, the experts preferred more flexible options (p. 1).
- Before this paper, evidence of the effect of scenario planning on managers' judgment was "almost nonexistent":
  the authors found three experiments, all with students (p. 2).
- The design was a before and after comparison; "it was not possible to have a control group" (p. 4).

## What it means for the plugin
- Scenarios are used to test choices and to prefer flexible ones, and are never reported as forecasts.
  `playbooks/scenarios.md` asks what holds in every scenario and what depends on one.
- One scenario read alone pulls judgment toward itself, in this study. The playbook has every option read against
  all the scenarios, as `playbooks/stress-test.md` does.
- Three workshops without a control group, on freight infrastructure. It shows what scenarios did to those
  experts' judgment; it does not show that their decisions were better.
