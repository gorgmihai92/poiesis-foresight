# Timid choices and bold forecasts: a cognitive perspective on risk taking (Kahneman & Lovallo, 1993)

- **Source:** Management Science 39(1): 17–31 · https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.39.1.17
- **File:** none (closed; no open copy listed)
- **Licence:** closed.
- **Evidence:** analytical · the abstract says the biases "are documented in psychological research".
- **Read:** abstract only, as OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar returned it on 2026-11-22 through poiesis-research's
  `search.py`.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- Decision makers "have a strong tendency to consider problems as unique" and "neglect the statistics of the
  past in evaluating current plans".
- "Overly optimistic forecasts result from the adoption of an inside view of the problem, which anchors
  predictions on plans and scenarios."

## What it means for the plugin
- It is the origin of the inside and outside view that `playbooks/base-rates.md` starts from. The steps of the
  playbook come from Flyvbjerg 2006, which was read in part and describes the method built on this idea.
