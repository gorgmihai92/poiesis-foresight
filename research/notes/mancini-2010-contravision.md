# Contravision (Mancini, Rogers, Bandara, Coe, Jędrzejczyk & Joinson, 2010)

- **Source:** full title "Contravision: exploring users' reactions to futuristic technology" · Proceedings of the
  SIGCHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems, 2010: 153–162 · https://doi.org/10.1145/1753326.1753350
- **File:** none (closed; no open copy listed)
- **Licence:** closed.
- **Evidence:** empirical · a user study with video scenarios of a personal healthcare technology (abstract). The
  sample is not in the abstract.
- **Read:** abstract only, as OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar returned it on 2026-11-22 through poiesis-research's
  `search.py`.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- The approach shows a positive and a negative version of the same scenarios of a proposed technology.
- "the use of two systematically comparable representations of the same technology can elicit a wider spectrum of
  reactions than a single representation can".

## What it means for the plugin
- `playbooks/scenarios.md` and `playbooks/challenge.md` ask for versions in contrast: the same future written to
  its advantage and to its cost. The study is about people's reactions to videos of one technology; that
  contrast helps a single analyst judge a scenario is this plugin's extension, said as such in the playbook.
