# Identifying and cultivating superforecasters as a method of improving probabilistic predictions (Mellers et al., 2015)

- **Source:** Perspectives on Psychological Science 10(3): 267–281 · https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691615577794
- **File:** none (closed; no open copy listed)
- **Licence:** closed.
- **Evidence:** empirical · forecasting tournaments sponsored by the US intelligence community (abstract).
- **Read:** abstract only, as OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar returned it on 2026-11-22 through poiesis-research's
  `search.py`.

## What it says (from the abstract)
- The best forecasters, picked each year and put in teams, "maintained high accuracy across hundreds of questions
  and a wide array of topics" two years in a row.
- The authors find support for four explanations: cognitive abilities and styles, task-specific skills,
  motivation and commitment, and enriched environments.
